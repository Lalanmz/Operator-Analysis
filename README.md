# 📊 ICTA Power BI Dashboard – Telecom Operator Insights
This Power BI project delivers an end-to-end analytical view of telecom operators’ customer satisfaction and network performance in Azerbaijan.  
The dataset combines continuous **online surveys** with **202 validated in-person responses**, creating a robust foundation for evaluating market perception, loyalty, and churn risk across major mobile operators.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective
To identify key satisfaction and dissatisfaction drivers, benchmark operator performance, and model churn risk using advanced DAX-based KPIs and dynamic Power BI visualizations.

---

## ⚙️ Tools & Methodology
- **Platform:** Microsoft Power BI (2023+)
- **Data Source:** Customer survey responses collected online and via field interviews
- **Process:** Power Query for transformation → DAX for analytical modeling → Power BI visuals for insights
- **Model:** A single unified fact table ensuring efficient filtering, aggregation, and relationship management across survey dimensions (operator, service type, response channel)

---

## 🧠 Analytical Approach
Advanced DAX measures were created to transform qualitative responses into quantitative business indicators, including:

- **Satisfaction Index**
- **Network Quality Score**
- **Customer Loyalty Rate**
- **Churn Risk Ratio**

These KPIs enabled segmentation by operator, demographic group, and response channel — turning perception data into actionable intelligence.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights & Business Impact
- **Operator Differentiation:** Each telecom brand demonstrates unique strengths — one leads in **network reliability**, another in **customer care**, while another lags due to **pricing concerns**.
- **Churn Risk Modeling:** DAX-driven “Risk %” measures show that **slow internet speed** and **frequent disconnections** are the main triggers for customer loss.
- **Satisfaction Drivers:** Weighted factor analysis indicates that **service quality** and **price fairness** are the strongest predictors of satisfaction — surpassing even network speed.
- **Channel Comparison:** **Online respondents** show higher satisfaction levels, highlighting stronger engagement among digital users.
- **Competitive Benchmarking:** Interactive visuals allow side-by-side operator comparisons across over **10 performance dimensions**, enabling real-time strategic insights.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Structure
1. **Executive Summary** – High-level KPIs and operator benchmarks  
2. **Internet Quality** – Network stability, speed, and performance metrics  
3. **Customer Satisfaction** – Key drivers and overall sentiment analysis  
4. **Churn Insights** – Risk segmentation and retention indicators  

---

## 🚀 Outcome
The project demonstrates expert-level use of **Power BI and DAX** to translate unstructured customer feedback into a data-driven intelligence system.  
By quantifying perception metrics and modeling churn probability, it empowers telecom operators to:

- Track real-time service performance  
- Detect emerging satisfaction risks  
- Prioritize improvements with measurable business impact
